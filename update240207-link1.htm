EMBASSY OF REPUBLIC SOUTH KOREA

Documents required for Tourist Visa

  • Valid passport
  • Visa application form duly filled in (photocopy form can be used)
  • 1 Passport size coloured photographs
  • Letter from the applicant stating purpose and duration of visit
  • Detailed tour itinerary
  • Tour confirmation & hotel confirmation
  • Confirmed round - trip air ticket
  • Sufficient foreign exchange endorsed on passport
  • Applicants have to provide their personal i/tax papers
  • Bank statement for last 6 months

Visa Fee: Rs. 1, 500/- (Payable in Cash)
Time Taken: Minimum 3 Working Days for processing after receipt of complete application.

Submission: Monday To Friday
Time: 09:00am To 11:00am

Collection: Monday To Friday
Time: 09:00am To 11:00am

 

Documents required for Business Visa

  • Valid passport
  • 1 Visa application form duly filled in (photocopy can be used)
  • 1 Photographs
  • Covering letter from the company stating applicant's name, designation, purpose and duration of visit
  • Invitation from korea to come to the embassy directly.
  • Registration copy of the Korean company.
  • Company profile in brief. .
  • I/Tax return of previous year.
  • Foreign exchange duly endorsed / copy of credit card
  • Confirmed round - trip air ticket / booking certificate.
Medical Requirements:
Nil
Visa Fee: Payable in Cash, Rs. 1, 500/-
Time Taken: Minimum 3 Working Days for processing after receipt of complete application.
Submission: Monday To Friday, 09:00am To 11:00am
Collection: Monday To Friday, 09:00am To 11:00am
