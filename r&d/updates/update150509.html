HONG KONG BUSINESS VISA - UPDATE
Dear Travel Partners,

You are aware that the Visa for Hong Kong upto a period of 14 days for Indian Nationals is obtained on arrival.The Processing time for visa was 2 working days. However, it has been ruled by the Embassy of China that due to some contraints an appointment have to be seaked for, to submit the application. This will normally take 3-4 working days.After obtaining the date the application will be submitted by us.
For your ready perusal, the Business visa requirements for Hong Kong are:
  • Passport with validity of more than six months.
  • 1 Visa application form duly filled in and signed-Photocopy can be used.
  • 2 Recent passport size color photographs with white background.
  • Original covering letter on the company's letter head from the applicant duly signed and typed with the company stamp address to - The Visa Officer, Embassy of China, New Delhi.
  • Invitation from Hong Kong Company with seal & signed.
  • Confirm Air Tickets.
