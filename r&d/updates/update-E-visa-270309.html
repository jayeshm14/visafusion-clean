E-VISA INTRODUCED-FLYING TO SINGAPORE BECOMES SIMPLER

SINGAPORE EMBASSY LAUNCHES E VISA

Dear Travel Partners,

With Effect from 01st April 2009,The embassy of Singapore will be introducing the E-VISA service. Applications will now be submitted online only and no applications will be submitted or collected from the Embassy counter. Travelers will now be required to carry the E-VISA at the time of travel.

 

 

 

For submission of your applications we will require the following documents-

1) Passport with atleast 6 months of Validity.
2) Duly filled and signed application form.
3) Recent passport size photograph (35mm X 45mm)without border,matt finish with 80% of the facial Coverage.

Once we have the above mentioned documents, the application will be made online and the Visa fee of $30(Singapore Dollar) will be paid. Once the fee is paid the print out of the approval copy or the E-visa will be taken out. The applicant will then be required to produce this copy of E-Visa upon arrival at the Singapore airport. Please note that we do not require the confirmed ticket copy for submitting the application online

Please do not submit any false information through your visa application form to us and note that the visa fee is non refundable and entering Singapore is on the sole discretion of the Immigration.
