UPDATE 28th February 2004
TO : MR. RAJAN DUA
DEAR TRAVEL PARTNERS,

AT THE OUTSET, WE ALL AT "UDAAN" PRESENT OUR BEST COMPLIMENTS TO YOU AND THANK YOU FOR YOUR VALUED SUPPORT AND PATRONISATION WHICH HAS RAISED US TO BE THE LEADING VISA FACILITATION AGENCY IN INDIA.

FRIENDS, RECENTLY WITH THE INTRODUCTION OF THE NEW ARRANGEMENT OF THE HIGH COMMISSION OF AUSTRALIA AND NEWZEALAND WE HAVE BEEN GIVEN TO UNDERSTAND THAT WITH EFFECT FROM 1ST MARCH 2004 PASSPORTS CAN BE PROCESSED AT THE LOCAL CENTERS OF T.T SERVICES AT NEW DELHI, MUMBAI, CHENNAI, KOLKATA AND HYDERABAD AS AUTHORISED BY THE RESPECTIVE MISSIONS.IT IS BELIEVED THAT THIS NEW SCHEME WILL ENABLE BETTER SERVICE TO THE TRAVELLING MASSES THEREBY AVOIDING UNNECESSARY TRAVEL AND INCONVENIENCE TO PROSPECTIVE VISITORS TO THESE COUNTRIES.

NEVERTHELESS, WE WISH TO BRING TO YOUR KIND ATTENTION THAT SUBMITTING AT THE LOCAL T.T. SERVICES OUTLETS WILL NOT BE A COMPULSION.YOUR APPLICATIONS CAN STILL CONTINUE TO COME TO OUR OFFICE FOR THE PRIMARY SCRUTINY OF THE CASES BEFORE SUBMISSION TO T.T SERVICES OFFICE AT NEW DELHI.

AS WE RECEIVE APPLICATIONS FOR AUSTRALIA AND NEWZEALAND FROM YOUR OFFICE WE WILL DO THE RUDIMENTARY SCREENING OF THE ATTACHMENTS AND SORT OUT THE NECESSARY SHORTCOMING IN EACH CASE THUS ONLY SUBMITTING COMPLETE AND PERFECT FILES TO THE HIGH COMMISSION.THE COLLECTION CAN BE EXPECTED ON THE FOURTH WORKING DAY AFTER SUBMISSION.
 * T.T. SERVICES FEE FOR AUSTRALIA RS 350/- PER APPLICATION
 * T.T. SERVICES FEE FOR NEWZEALAND NIL (TILL A NOTICE IS ISSUED)

ABOVE AMOUNT WILL BE IN ADDITION TO OUR HANDLING AND COURIER CHARGES

WE WILL ENSURE THAT DOCUMENTS REACH THE MISSIONS ON TIME AND THE PROCESSING TIME BE MINIMISED.WE WOULD ALSO DO THE FOLLOW UP ON EACH CASE DAILY THROUGH THEIR ENQUIRY SECTION WHICH WILL BE JUST A LOCAL AWAY.YOUR STATUS WILL BE UPDATED BY OUR EMAILS ON A REGULAR BASIS THUS MAINTAINING THE STREAM AS WE HAVE BEEN WORKING IN CLOSE COORDINATION WITH YOUR OFFICE AND HAVE CONTRIBUTED TOWARDS VISA ISSUANCE FOR YOUR ORGANISATION FOR OVER A DECADE NOW.

WE, THEREFORE, WITH THE ABOVE IN VIEW, ANTICIPATE YOUR CONTINUED SUPPORT TO COORDINATE EFFACTUATION OF QUALITY APPLICATIONS TO THE MISSIONS AND RENDERING OF AGILE SERVICES TO OUR VALUED CLIENTS.
