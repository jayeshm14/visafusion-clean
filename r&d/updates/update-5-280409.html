CHANGE OF NAME AFTER MARRIAGE

  1. Fresh Form No. 1 to be filled and signed.
  2. Four colour photos with white background passport size.
  3. Copy of marriage certificate duly self attested or Annexure-D duly notarized.
  4. Copy of proof of residence self or your husband like:
    1. MTNL/BSNL telephone bill.
    2. Election Identity Card.
    3. Electricity bill.
    4. House tax receipts.
    5. Rent Agreement/Lease-deed.
    6. Driving Licence.
    7. Gas connection receipt.
    8. Pass-book of any nationalized bank.
  5. Copy of passport of your husband first four pages and last four pages duly self attested.
