SINGAPORE- Enhancement Of Visa Charges including Visa fee

Dear Travel Partners,

As you all know that Singapore visa will be a paper visa from 01 April'09 onwards. This type of visa involves an extra effort to scrutinize the documents for their authentication & further maintain records at our end for a period of 1 year after the expiry of visa. This will involve an extra cost to meet the logistics of the same.
Therefore, in view of the above, We have revised the visa charges to Rs. 1,300/- per application including visa fee. We, sincerely hope for your cooperation.
