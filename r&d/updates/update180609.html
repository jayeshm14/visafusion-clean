BANGLADESH - UPDATE

Dear Travel Partners,

It is bought to your kind notice that Bangladesh Business Visa requirements are changed.The changes are mentioned below:

 
The Business visa requirements for Bangladesh are:
  • Passport with validity of more than six months.
  • 2 Visa application forms duly filled in and signed- Photocopy can be used
  • 2 Recent passport size colour photographs with a white background to be glued at the appropriate place on the visa application form.
  • Covering letter from the applicant on company's letter head stating his name, designation, purpose and duration of visit.
  • Original Invitation Letter from Bangladesh Stating Applicant's Name, Designation, Purpose & Duration of Visit (In case of fax kindly make sure that Bangladesh fax no is mentioned).
  • A recommendation letter from the recognized Chamber of Commerce is mandatory.
  • Confirmed return Air Ticket. (Not Mandatory).
