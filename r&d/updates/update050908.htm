UDAAN - UPDATE 5th September 2008.
 
CHANGE IN PHOTO SPECIFICATIONS FOR FRENCH VISA
With immediate effect the photo specification for visa to France has changed. Applicants will now have to submit the photograph with a size of 35 x 45 mm, white background, 40% of face with shoulders visible.
 
BIOMETRIC SYSTEM INTRODUCED FOR VISA TO MEXICO
Applicants traveling to MEXICO are now required to come personally for submission of their applications in New Delhi. During the submission applicants will be required to undergo the BIOMETRIC SYSTEM similar to the UK VFS process. However we can assist you in documentation.
 
CHANGE IN USA VISA FEE

Kindly note that with immediate effect the VISA FEE has changed. The new details are as follows-

Visa Fee of Rs 6026 payable by draft favoring “US EMBASSY-Visa Fees”- New Delhi

VFS Service Charge Rs 322 payable by draft favoring “HDFC BANK A/C VFS”

 
VISA TO CHINA TO BE SERVICED BY VFS
You all might be aware that visa to china is now being processed through VFS.Please note that the visa fee & VFS charges are non-refundable once the case is applied and rejection of visa is on the sole discretion of the embassy. The status of visa can only be updated once the passport is released from the VFS.
 
ATTENTION TRAVELERS TO GREECE

Note that travelers applying for a Business Visa are now required to submit the following documents in addition to the existing one:-

1) Company's & Personal Updated Bank Statements of the last 3 months.

2) Company's & Personal ITR of the last 3 years.

3) Hotel is now required on the Hotel Letter Head, a copy of which should be directly faxed to us from Greece.
