CAPITAL : CANBERRA ( CBR )

TRANSIT VISA

· Passport valid for minimum of 6 months
· 1 visa application Form 876 duly filled in and signed
If in transit for less than 8 hours en route New Zealand
· Tour Itinerary
· Valid onward visa to New Zealand
If in transit for more than 72 hour en route any other destination or in transit to New Zealand for more than 8 hours
· 1 Form M-67 to be duly filled with family details and signed
· 1 recent passport size colour photograph
· Covering letter on the business letterhead stating the applicant's name, address, designation, purpose and duration of stay
· Detailed tour itinerary and tour confirmation
· Hotel confirmation
· Airline booking certificate
· If going for Employment to other destinations, then a copy of the Work Permit and visa approval from the Government of the country
· Proof of financial solvency - liquid funds (if available), copies of property papers (if any), fixed deposits or investments (if any)
· Bank statements for the last 6 months
· Income Tax Returns for the last 2 years
· Proof of occupation:
1) Employed / Service
· Salary slips
· Leave letter from the employer
2) Self - Employed / Business
· Proof of company registration / company profile
· Proof of ownership
. Private limited company - articles of association
. Proprietorship company - letter from bankers
. Partnership company - partnership deed, bank statements for the last 6 months and i / tax papers for the last 3 years
3) Students
· Leave certificate from school or vacation circular.
4) Individuals / children traveling alone
· Consent letter in support to the visit from both the parents / non-traveling spouse
· Passport copies of both parents to confirm signatures
· In absence of passport copy, consent letter to be given on Rs. 2 /- stamp paper and attested by the notary public
· Parents' proof of financial solvency and proof of occupation, in detail.
5) Applicant above 65 years
· Medical (travel) Insurance
· 4 recent passport size colour photographs

Health Requirements: Medical (Travel) Insurance for applicant above 65 years of age

Fee: Rs.2, 200 /- payable by demand draft drawn in favour of "Australian High Commission" - New Delhi And an additional amount of Rs. 450/- payable by cash as a TTS service charge

Time Taken: 5 working days
