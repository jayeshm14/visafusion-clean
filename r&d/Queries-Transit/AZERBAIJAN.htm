TRANSIT VISA

For a Single Entry Transit Visa up to 7 days, travelers do not need to obtain a visa, prior to travel. The visa can be obtained on arrival from the visa section of the Consular Department of Azerbaijan's Ministry of Foreign Affairs in the H. International Airport of Baku. The list of documents required is: · Valid passport for minimum of 6 months
· Visa Application Form
· 2 recent passport size colour photographs
· Visa Fee of US $ 40 payable in cash
