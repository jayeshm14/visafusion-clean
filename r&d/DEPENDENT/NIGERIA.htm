CAPITAL : ABUJA ( ABV )

DEPENDENT VISA

. Passport valid for 6 months along with photocopies of 1st, 2nd and last page.
. 4 Visa application form duly filled in and signed.

Please note: Children below 16 years of age are required to complete 2 forms only. And also, children above 16 should indicate as what work will they be engaged in, after arrival at Nigeria.

. 4 recent passport size colour photographs.
. Covering Letter stating the applicant's name, address, designation, purpose and duration of the stay
. Invitation / Sponsorship / Consent Letter from the father or husband, on his company letterhead.
. Attested copies of the husband's / father's permanent residence permit copy
. Marriage Certificate, if applicable
. Birth Certificate, if applicable
. Security Clearance for adults

Medical Requirement: Yellow Fever Certificate required for travel.

Fee: Rs.5, 500 /- payable by either cash or demand draft drawn in favour of 'Nigerian High Commission' - New Delhi.

Please note, Double visa fee in case of an urgent visa.

Time Taken: 48 hours for Normal Visa
Same day for Urgent visa
