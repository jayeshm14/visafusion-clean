CAPITAL : AMMAN ( AMM )

BUSINESS

· Valid passport for minimum of 6 months
· Visa application form duly filled in and signed.
· 1 recent passport size colour photograph.
· Covering letter stating the applicant's name, address, contact number, designation, purpose and duration of stay.
· Sponsorship letter from the counterpart Jordanian company.
· Invitation message should come directly from the Home Ministry of the Hashmite Kingdom of Jordan to the embassy at New Delhi by fax and a copy to us.
· Original Bank Statements for the last 1 year
· Original registration certificate of company, in case of applicant applying through the company
· Letter from the Indian Chamber of Commerce

Health requirement: Nil.

Fee: Rs.935 /- for single entry visa Rs.1, 785 /- for multiple entry visa

Time taken: To be applied at least 2 weeks prior to date of travel

Note: Personal presence of applicant might be required in certain cases, which is solely dependent on the discretion of the visa counselor.

Note: An authority letter in favour of "Udaan" has to be issued by the applicant along with all the documents, for processing and follow up of the application.
