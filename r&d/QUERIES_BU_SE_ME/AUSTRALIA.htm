CAPITAL : CANBERRA ( CBR )

BUSINESS

· Passport valid for minimum of 6 months
· 1 visa application Form 456 duly filled in and signed
· 1 Form M-67 to be duly filled with family details and signed
· 1 recent passport size colour photograph
· Covering letter on the business letterhead stating the applicant's name, address, designation, purpose and duration of stay
· Invitation letter on the company letterhead from the counterpart Australian company
· Copy of correspondence / business transaction with the Australian counterpart or evidence of any fixed appointments
· Brief company profile
· Company bank statements for the last 6 months.
· Company Income Tax Returns for the last 2 years
· Foreign exchange endorsements on passport / copy of credit card.

Incase of a first time traveler to Australia, the following additional documents are required:

· Copy of the appointment letter, which should contain the date of joining, designation and the salary drawn.
· Copies of recent salary slips
· All relevant educational and professional certificates
