No Representation in India But Indian national require a visa; they need to apply for a visa through Netherlands Embassy.
"The Royal Netherlands Embassy" - New Delhi is situated at
6/50-F, Shantipath, Chanakyapuri, New Delhi
Phone: 91-11-26884951/52/53/54
E-mail: nde@minbuza.nl
The documents required for the Visa Application procedure is same as that of Netherlands.


· Passport valid for minimum of 6 months
· 1 recent passport size colour photograph
· Covering Letter mentioning the applicant's name, address, designation, purpose and duration of stay
· Invitation Letter from the concerned company at Aruba.
· Financial solvency proof - bank statements copies, credit card copies, Income Tax papers, etc
· Confirmed air ticket

Please note, additional requirements may be required as per the visa application

Fee: Rs.1, 260 /- payable by cash (for a visit of 30 days)
Please note, visa fee generally vary on the duration of the stay and can only be confirmed from the embassy as per application

Time Taken: The procedure takes about four weeks or more, since the application has to be first forwarded to the Ministry in Holland and then to the Ministry in Aruba.

Please note, applicant could be called in for a personal presence in certain cases; this would depend on the sole discretion of the High Commission. In that case, an appointment has to be taken from the Netherlands Embassy; the appointment can be obtained through telephone # 91-11-55687000 from Monday to Thursday between 10.00 hours to 16:00 hours and Fridays between 10.00 hours to 14:00 hours
