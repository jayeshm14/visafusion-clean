FOR THE APPOINTMENT YOU HAVE THREE OPTIONS:-
1.) BETWEEN 09:00 -10:00 A.M 2.) BETWEEN 10:00 - 11:00 A.M 3.) BETWEEN 11:00 - 12:00 A.M IT IS ESSENTIAL THAT YOU COME IN TIME ACCORDING TO THE FIXED APPOINTMENT.
