"THE EMBASSY OF THE STATE OF KUWAIT" - NEW DELHI DOES NOT ISSUE A BUSINESS VISA. A VISA TO ENTER THE COUNTRY IS ARRANGED BY THE COUNTERPART INVITING COMPANY IN KUWAIT AND AN APPROVAL LETTER ALONG WITH ALONG WITH THE "NO OBJECTION CERTIFICATE" ISSUED BY THE CHAMBER OF COMMERCE OF KUWAIT, IN REFERENCE TO THE ABOVE IS SENT TO THE APPLICANT. APPLICANT TRAVELS WITH THIS LETTER AND GETS VISA ON ARRIVAL AT THE PORT.
