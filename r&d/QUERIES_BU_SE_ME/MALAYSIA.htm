CAPITAL : KUALA LUMPUR ( KUL )

THE FEDERATION OF MALAYSIA INCLUDES : THE STATES OF MALAYSIA AND THE BORNEO STATES : SARAWAK AND SABAH.

BUSINESS

Manual visa application

. Passport valid for minimum of 6 months
· Visa Application form duly filled in and signed
· 2 recent passport size colour photographs with white background
· Covering Letter stating the applicant's name, designation, purpose and duration of stay
· Invitation Letter from the counterpart Please note, the Invitation Letter should not mention any other term except the word 'conference' otherwise an approval would be required
· Financial Solvency Proof - Bank Statements for the last 6 months, Income Tax Papers for the last 3 years
· Foreign Exchange Endorsement / Copy of credit card
· Confirmed Return Air-ticket.

Medical Requirement: Nil

Fee: Rs.650 /- for single entry Rs.1, 300 /- for multiple entries

Please note, All fee payable in demand draft drawn in favour of the 'Malaysian High Commission' payable in New Delhi

Time Taken: 3 working days

Online I-Visa application (only for single entry)

· Passport valid for minimum of 6 months
· 2 recent passport size colour photographs with white background
· Covering Letter stating the applicant's name, designation, purpose and duration of stay along with the flight details - flight number and travel date

Medical Requirement: Nil

Fee: Rs.650 /- for single entry

Please note, fee payable in demand draft drawn in favour of the 'Malaysian High Commission' payable in New Delhi

Time Taken: 24 hours for the visa approval and 1 working day for Visa Stamping
