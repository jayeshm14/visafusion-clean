TOURIST

· Original Passport including the photocopies of the relevant Visa pages.
· 1 Original Visa application form along with Instruction / Information sheet duly filled in with Type Writer or Computer Printer only and signed.
· 2 Recent passport size colour photographs to be glued on the appropriate place on the visa application form.
· Covering letter from the applicant stating his name, designation, purpose and duration of visit.
· Hotel booking and detailed tour itinerary
· Personal Income Tax papers/ TDS for the last three years.
· Personal Bank Statement for the last three months.
· Copy of the International Credit Card
· Confirmed return Air Ticket.

Note: - Please note, if the applicant's visa request is approved, the applicant would be issued the Visa Approval Document. With this the applicant can travel to Malta. Upon the presentation of this Visa Approval Document to the Immigration Authorities at the Malta Airport, the visa will be endorsed on the passport.

HEALTH REQUIREMENT: Nil

Visa Fee: - Payable in cash (The Visa fee would be charged at Malta airport upon arrival)

Fee Type : -

1.) Tourist Visa (Single Entry) 10 Maltese Pounds (LM)
2.) Transit Visa 5 Maltese Pounds (LM)
3.) Tourist Visa (Multiple Entry) 20 Maltese Pounds (LM)

Processing Fee: - Rs. 950/-Payable by cash (to be paid at the time of Submission of the applicant) Kindly note that this is not the visa fee; it is only the processing fee payable to the consulate.

Time Taken: - 15 business days for processing after receipt of complete application.

Submission: - Monday to Friday
Time: - 11:00am to 12:30pm

Collection: - As per the receipt of the approval

Note: - Further, any change in fees, additional document or personal presence of the applicant might be required which is on the discretion of the embassy.
