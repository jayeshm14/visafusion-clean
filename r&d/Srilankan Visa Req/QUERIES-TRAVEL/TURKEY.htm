TOURIST

· Passport should be valid for at least 6 months from the date of travel.
· 2 original Visa application forms duly filled in and signed
· 2 Recent passport size colour photographs with light background.
· Covering letter from the applicant's company stating his name, designation, purpose and duration of visit.
· Hotel Confirmation.
· Foreign exchange duly endorsed @ USD 50 per day.
· Confirmed round trip air tickets.

For First Time Traveler: -
· Applicant's personal bank statement for the last six months
· Applicant's personal IT papers for the last three years
