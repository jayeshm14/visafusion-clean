TOURIST

· Passport should be valid for at least 6 months , in case of renewal of passport, the previous passport should be enclosed in its presentation for Visa · 2 Original Visa application forms duly filled in and signed
· 2 Recent passport size colour photographs with light background.
· Covering letter from the applicant's company stating his name, designation, purpose and duration of visit.
· Hotel confirmation along with the tour itinerary
· Incase of the sponsored trips, Sponsor in Peru or Srilanka should be equally accredited for his / her financial status, employment and his/ hers source of income or sponsor should present a written affidavit duly signed and certified by the notary, in which he/ she states that he/ she shall be responsible to bear all costs of boarding and lodging of the applicant or repatriation in case of traveler has acquired an illegal status or does not have sufficient funds to return, at the moment of presenting the document the applicant should sign the same.
· Company profile and the proof of registration off the company in which the applicant is employed.
· Proof of financial documents like latest attested copy of individual Income Tax Receipts for the last 3 years, Bank Statement for the last 6 months, Property papers.
· Foreign Exchange must be endorsed in the passport.
· Confirmed round trip air tickets

Kindly note that "In no case photocopies/ facsimile copies of the documents would be accepted".
