TOURIST

· Passport should be valid for at least 6 months from the date of travel.
· 1 Visa application form duly filled in and signed.
· 2 Recent passport size colour photographs.
· Covering letter from the applicants stating his name, designation, purpose and duration of visit.
· Hotel conformation by an established travel agency.
· Tour Itinerary.
· Proof of financial status/ Credit card copy.
· Confirmed round trip air tickets.

HEALTH REQUIREMENT: Nil

Visa Fee: - Rs. 1435/- payable in cash

Time Taken: - 8-10 business days for processing after receipt of complete application, as documents are referred for visa authorization to Tunis.
To get this approval these are the following ways:
1.) After the documents have been submitted at the Embassy , the embassy sends the application to the Ministry of Home Affairs - Tunisia, and gets the approval
2.) The counter part arranges this from the Ministry of Home Affairs - Tunisia and faxes it directly to the Embassy here.

Submission: - Monday to Friday
Time: - 9:00 am to 12:30pm

Collection: - Monday to Friday
Time: - Depends upon the receipt of approval from Tunisia

Embassy generally does not entertain the Tourist Visa.

Note: - Further, any change in fees, additional document or personal presence of the applicant might be required which is on the discretion of the embassy.
