TOURIST

· Valid passport with validity of more than six months.
· 1 Original Visa application form duly filled in and signed.
· 2 Recent passport size colour photographs with light background to be glued on the appropriate place on the visa application form.
· Covering letter from the applicant stating his name, designation, purpose and duration of visit.
· Hotel confirmation along with the copy of the paid voucher of the hotel booking.
· Personal Income Tax papers/ TDS for the last three years.
· Personal Bank Statement for the last three months.
· Copy of the International Credit Card
· Confirmed return Air Ticket.

Time Taken: - 15 business days for processing after receipt of complete application as the application goes for approval.

Submission: - Monday to Friday
Time: - 10:00am to 11:00am
