TOURIST

Kindly note that the Embassy Of The Syrian Arab Republic generally doesn't issue the Tourist Visa, they verify the documents first and the final discretion lies with the Embassy.

· Valid passport with validity for more than six months.
· 3 Visa application form duly filled in and signed- Photocopy can be used
· 3 Recent passport size colour photographs with light background to be glued on the appropriate place on the visa application form.
· Covering letter from the applicant on company's letter head stating his name, designation, purpose and duration of visit.
· Hotel conformation and tour itinerary.
· Recommendation letter from the Ministry Of External Affairs/ Foreign Affairs stating the applicant's credentials.
· Personal Income Tax papers/ TDS for the last three years.
· Personal Bank Statement for the last three months.
· Copy of the International Credit Card
· Original confirmed return Air Ticket.

HEALTH REQUIREMENT: Nil

Visa Fee: - payable in cash
For first time traveler (fresh passport), the applicant will have to pay a fees of Rs. 1,600/-(Single Entry)

Fee Type : -

1.) Single Entry Rs. 9,50/-
2.) Multiple Entry Rs. 3,200/-

Time Taken: - 3 business days for processing after receipt of complete application

Submission: - Monday to Friday
Time: - 11:00am to 12:00noon

Collection: - Monday to Friday
Time: - 11:00am to 12:00noon

Note: - Further, any change in fees, additional document or personal presence of the applicant might be required which is on the discretion of the embassy.
