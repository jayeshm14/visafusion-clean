Tanzanian Visa is a Referred Visa. The procedure takes around one month for the approval of visa. On the receipt of the visa approval, the applicant is required to submit the documents at the embassy to obtain the visa.

Visa requirements to initiate the process:-

In this first stage of the processing of the visa, the application is referred to Tanzania for approval of visa. This process will take around a month's time.
· 2 Visa application form duly filled in and signed.
· 2 Recent passport size colour photographs.

TOURIST

These documents needs to be submitted to the embassy on the receipt of the visa approval from Tanzania

· Passport should be valid for at least 6 months from the date of travel.
· Covering letter from the applicant stating his name, designation, purpose and duration of visit.
· Hotel confirmation and Tour Itinerary.
· Proof of financial status/ Credit card copy/ Foreign Exchange endorsement for minimum of 1000 USD
· Confirmed round trip air tickets.

HEALTH REQUIREMENT: Nil

Visa Fee: - Rs. 9,700/-- payable in cash

Time Taken: - 2 business days for processing after receipt visa approval.

Submission: - Monday to Friday
Time: - 10:00am to 12:00noon

Collection: - Monday to Friday
Time: - 3:30pm to 4:00pm

Note: - Further, any change in fees, additional document or personal presence of the applicant might be required which is on the discretion of the embassy.
