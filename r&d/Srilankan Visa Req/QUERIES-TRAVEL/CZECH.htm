TOURIST

· Passport should be valid for at least 6 months from the date of travel.
· 1 Original Visa application form duly filled in and signed
· 2 Recent passport size colour photographs to be glued on the appropriate place on the visa application form.
· Covering letter from the applicant stating his name, designation, purpose and duration of visit.
· Hotel booking and detailed tour itinerary
· Proof of foreign Exchange funds for the trip.
· Personal Income Tax papers/ TDS for the last three years.
· Personal Bank Statement for the last three months.
· Copy of the International Credit Card
· Confirmed return Air Ticket.

Medical Requirements: - Medical Insurance for the stay period.

Visa Fee: - Payable by cash

Fee Type : -

1.) Single Entry ( 90 days) Rs. 2,200/-
2.) Multiple Entry (90 days) Rs. 5,500/-

Time Taken: - 3 business days for processing after receipt of complete application.

Submission: - Monday to Thursday
Time: - 9:00 am to 11:00pm

Collection: - Monday to Friday
Time: - 4:00pm

Note:- Please enclose the photocopies of the passport ( 1, 2 and last page along with all the valid visas)

Note: - Further, any change in fees, additional document or personal presence of the applicant might be required which is on the discretion of the embassy.
