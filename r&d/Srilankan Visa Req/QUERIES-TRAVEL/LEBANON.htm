TOURIST

· Original Passport with minimum 6 months of validity along with 2 sets of photocopies of all the relevant pages of the passport
· 2 Visa application form duly filled in and signed.
· 2 Recent passport size colour photographs to be glued on the appropriate place on the visa application form.
· Covering letter from the applicant stating his name, designation, purpose and duration of visit.
· An original legalized sponsorship letter, addressed to the Embassy to be faxed directly to the Embassy from the person to be visited in Lebanon.
· Leave certificate from the employer.
· Proof of dependent family members, job, business or any kind of establishment.
· Hotel Confirmation and tour Itinerary.
· Personal Income Tax papers/ TDS for the last three years.
· Personal Bank Statement for the last three months.
· Copy of the International Credit Card.
· Confirmed return Air Ticket.
