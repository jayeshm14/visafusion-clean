TOURIST

· Passport should be valid for at least 3 months after the date of exit form Bulgaria.
· 1 Visa application forms duly filled in and signed
· 2 Recent passport size colour photographs with light background.
· Covering letter from the applicant stating his name, designation, purpose and duration of visit.
· Original Invitation/ Declaration issued by a Bulgarian national in a standard form, attested by a Bulgarian Notary Public and by the local Bulgarian Municipal Authorities.
· Original document/ voucher issued by a Tourist Organization/ Tour Operator for Pre-Paid tourist services. The voucher should explicitly contain the license number of the Tourist Organization/ Tour Operator, issued by the Bulgarian Ministry Of Economy. The name and the license no of the hotel, and the duration of the pre-paid tour services.
· Recommendation letter from the Chamber Of Commerce- Srilanka
· Confirmed round trip air tickets

Note: - The applicant is advised to carry with him the original Invitation from his official/ Business/ Private partner in Bulgaria as the Bulgarian Immigration Officials at the border check point may ask to see it.

HEALTH REQUIREMENT: 1.) All applicants whose intended stay in Bulgaria is up to 90 days or who are passing transit through Bulgaria have to present along with all above documents an Insurance Certificate for an overseas Insurance, covering the Medical Expenses for the duration for the stay in Bulgaria.
2.) The applicants whose intended stay in Bulgaria is for a person longer than 30 days have to submit Medical test for HIV/ Aids Virus Certified by the Ministry Of Externals Affairs- Colombo
