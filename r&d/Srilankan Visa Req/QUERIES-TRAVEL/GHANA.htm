TOURIST

· Valid passport with validity of more than six months.
· 4 Visa application form duly filled in and signed- Photocopy can be used
· 4 Recent passport size colour photographs with light background to be glued on the appropriate place on the visa application form.
· Covering letter from the applicant stating his name, designation, purpose and duration of visit.
· Hotel Confirmation and tour Itinerary.
· Personal Income Tax papers/ TDS for the last three years.
· Personal Bank Statement for the last three months.
· Copy of the International Credit Card
· Confirmed return Air Ticket.

Medical Requirements: - Yellow fever vaccination - not mandatory for submission of application but is required for travel.

Visa Fee: - payable by demand draft (D/D) favoring the "Ghana High Commission" - New Delhi

Fee Type : -

1.) Single Entry Rs. 1,920/-
2.) Multiple Entry Rs. 3,840/-

Time Taken: - 3 business days for processing after receipt of complete application.

Submission: - Monday to Friday
Time: - 9:00am to 11:00am

Collection: - Monday to Friday
Time : - 9:00am to 11:00am

Note: - Further, any change in fees, additional document or personal presence of the applicant might be required which is on the discretion of the embassy.
