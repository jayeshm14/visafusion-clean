TOUSIST

· Passport should be valid for at least three months after the end of the validity of the visa and it must contain at least two blank pages for visa stamping.
· 1 Visa application form duly filled in and signed (photocopy can be used)
· 1 Recent passport size colour photograph, 35-40 mm in width taken against light back ground.
· Covering letter from the applicant stating his name, designation, purpose and duration of visit.
· Documents confirming the purpose and cause of the visit, like Voucher from a registered tourist/ travel agency
· Documents confirming that accommodation and other expenses will be covered when staying in Estonia
· Proof of financial status- Bank statements for the last three months (applicant must prove that they have adequate financial means, equivalent to Rs. 2000/- INR, for every day that they plan to stay in Estonia.
· Travel health insurance policy valid in Estonia, with a coverage of at least 10,226 Euro for the entire duration of the stay in Estonia.
· Confirmed round trip air tickets

Kindly note that all the documents should be deposited in the embassy in original either in Estonian or in English, and the personal presence of the applicant is Mandatory.

Medical Requirements: - Nil

Visa Fee: -The fee depends on the number of entries, the duration of the stay in Estonia and the validity of Visa.

Fee Type : -

1.) Single Entry Stay up to 30 days Rs. 1050
2.) Single Entry Stay up to 90 days Rs. 1840
3.) Multiple Entry Stay up to 30 days Rs. 1320
4.) Multiple Entry Stay up to 90 days Rs. 1840
5.) Multiple Entry Stay up to 1 year Rs. 2630

Time Taken: - Minimum of 7 business days and maximum of 30 business days for processing after receipt of complete application. (The number of days of processing depends from application to application)

Submission: - Monday to Friday
Time: - 8:30 am to 11:30am

Collection: - Monday to Friday
Time : - 3:30 pm to 4:30pm

Note: - Further, any change in fees, additional document or personal presence of the applicant might be required which is on the discretion of the embassy.
