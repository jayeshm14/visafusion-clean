TOURIST

The Republic of South Africa is a member of the Commonwealth and is comprised of the following states: Northern Province, North-West Province, Gauteng, Free State, Kwazulu Natal, Eastern Cape, Western Cape, Northern Cape and Mpumalanga.

· Passport should be valid for not less than 30 days after the expiry of intended visit.
· 1 Visa application form duly filled in and signed (photocopy can be used)
· 2 Recent passport size colour photographs.
· Covering letter from the applicant stating his name, designation, purpose and duration of visit. (if self employed covering letter is required on business letter head and if employed, same can be given on the plain paper)
· If employed, NOC from the company
· If student, NOC from school.
· Hotel confirmation.
· Detailed day to day tour itinerary.
· Proof of financial status-Income Tax papers for last three years/ Bank statements for the last three months/ Credit card copy/ Travelers cheque copy (if any)
· A cash deposit of Rs. 40,000/- INR-(refundable) may be asked for, from the Embassy on case to case basis.
· Confirmed round trip air tickets (ticket printout will also do)

If the applicant has relatives/ friends in South Africa with whom he would be staying, and who would be sponsoring the applicants visit, the following would be the additional requirements: Kindly note in case of sponsorship, no hotel conformation would be required.
· Invitation from the sponsor on their Business letterhead
· Passport Copy of the sponsor with visa pages
· Proof of relationship
· Resident I-Card copy
· Proof of financial documents

HEALTH REQUIREMENT: Nil, however Yellow Fever Certificate would be required if the journey starts or entails passing through the Yellow Fever belt of Africa or South America.

Visa Fee: - Rs. 2,125/- payable by demand draft (D/D) favoring the "South African High Commission" - New Delhi

Time Taken: - 4-5 business days for processing after receipt of complete application.

Submission: - Monday to Friday
Time: - 8:30 am to 11:30am

Collection: - Monday to Friday
Time: - 3:30 pm to 4:30pm

Note: - Further, any change in fees, additional document or personal presence of the applicant might be required which is on the discretion of the embassy.

Kindly note that in case where the applicant is required to pay 40,000/- as security at the Embassy, for the refund of the same, the applicant will have to give the following details to the Embassy:-
1. Account Name
2. Account No.
3. Type of account
4. Bank's name
5. Branch's name
6. Bank's address
7. Bank's No.
8. Bank's correspondent bank's address & name
9. Swift Code No. 10. Declaration in writing that $ 30 can be deducted from their account as processing charges for the Demand Draft
