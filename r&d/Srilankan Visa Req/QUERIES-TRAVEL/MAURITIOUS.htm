TOURIST

Mauritius High Commission issues a Gratis Visa

· Original Passport with minimum 6 months of validity
· 2 Visa application form duly filled in and signed.
· 2 Recent passport size colour photographs to be glued on the appropriate place on the visa application form.
· Covering letter from the applicant stating his name, designation, purpose and duration of visit and details of the company in Libya
· Hotel confirmation with Tour Itinerary.
· Foreign Exchange duly endorsed on the passport @ 50 USD per day or copy of the International credit card.
· Confirm return air ticket


HEALTH REQUIREMENT: Nil

Visa Fee: - Nil, Mauritius High Commission issues a Gratis Visa

Time Taken: - 24 hrs.

Submission: - Monday to Friday
Time: - 9:30 am to 12:30 pm

Collection: - Next Day
Time : - 3:00pm to 4:00pm

Note: - Further, any change in fees, additional document or personal presence of the applicant might be required which is on the discretion of the embassy.
