TRANSIT

· Passport should be valid for at least 3 months after the date of exit form Bulgaria.
· 1Visa application forms duly filled in and signed
· 2 Recent passport size colour photographs with light background.
· Covering letter from the applicant stating his name, designation, purpose and duration of visit.
· Onward ticket reservation and entry visa for the next country.
· In case of Seamen, letter of guarantee from a shipping company, letter from yteh shipping agent in Bulgaria giving particulars of the Seamen, date and port of Joining the ship, valid Seamen book and ticket.


HEALTH REQUIREMENT: Nil

Visa Fee: - To be paid in cash

This visa is valid up to 12 months from the day of issue for single/ double/ multiple transits with a maximum stay of 24 hrs per visit

Fee Type :-
1.) Transit Visa 1,100/-
2.) Double Transit 3 Months 2,200/-
3.) Double Transit 6 Months 2,750/-
4.) Multiple Transit 3 Months 3,300/-
5.) Multiple Transit 6 Months 3,580/-
6.) Multiple Entry 12 Months 4,950/-

Processing Fee :- Rs. 1,100/- (Non-Refundable) per visa.

Time Taken: - 10 business days for processing after receipt of complete application

Submission: - Monday to Thursday
Time: - 10:00 to 12:00noon

Collection: - Monday to Thursday
Time: - 10:00 to 12:00noon
