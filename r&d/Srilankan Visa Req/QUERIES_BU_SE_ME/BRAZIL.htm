· Passport with a minimum validity of at least 6 months from the date of travel along with the photocopy of the first two pages and the last page of the passport.
· Visa application form duly filled in (Back to back) and signed only with Blue Ink.
· Recent passport size (4x5cms) colour photographs with light background.
· Detailed covering letter from the applicant's company on the company's business letter head mentioning the applicants name, designation, purpose and duration of visit along with the following details:-
1. All expenditures of the applicant during the visit would be borne by the company.
2. The purpose of travel should be mentioned in details for example, if the applicant is going for Market assessment or for Import/ Export, the application should mention the same and not just Business Discussions.
3. The signing authority of the covering letter should mention his name/ designation/ contact phone number clearly.
4. The covering letter should have the stamp of the company also.
· Invitation from Brazil Counterpart should be faxed directly to the Embassy, mentioning the registration number of the company along with all the details of the visit including the duration of stay in Brazil.
· Recommendation letter from the Honorary Consul Of Brazil in Colombo.
· An endorsement letter from the "Chambers of Commerce" - Colombo or the Export Promotion Council - Colombo or "Manufactures Association" with which the applicants company has been registered with; mentioning that the applicant is an employee of the said company and that the association has no objections in his going to Brazil for Business.
· Foreign exchange duly endorsed/ Copy of credit card
· Confirmed Return Air Ticket
Kindly note that if the applicant is going for Training or for Work, he would be issued either Type-2 Visa or Type -5 Visa. In addition to the above mentioned documents, he will have to submit the following also:
· Detailed covering letter mentioning the day today agenda of the training
· One more additional Visa application form duly filled in (Back to back) and signed only with Blue Ink.
· One more additional recent passport size (4x5cms) colour photographs with light background.
· Original Police Clearance Certificate, either issued by the Passport Office or Local Police
HEALTH REQUIREMENT: A yellow fever vaccination certificate is requested if the applicant traveled within the last 90 days to Angola, Benin, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of Congo, Gabon, Gambia, Ghana, Guinea, Liberia, Nigeria, Siera Leone, Sudan, Bolivia, Colombia, Ecuador, French Guyana, Peru and Venezuela.
· Yellow fever vaccination is advisable if applicant's destination in Brazil includes any of the following States: Acre, Amazonas, Amapá, Federal District, Goiás, Maranhão, Mato Grosso, Mato Grosso do Sul, Pará, Rondônia, Roraima and Tocantins.

Visa Fee: - Rs. 3,500/- payable by demand draft (D/D) favoring the "Embassy Of Brazil" - New Delhi
Time Taken: - 3 business days for processing after receipt of complete application.

Submission: - Monday to Friday
Time: - 9:30am to 11:00pm
Collection: - After three working days from the day of the submission
Time: - 9:30am to 11:00pm
Note: - Further, any change in fees, additional document or personal presence of the applicant might be required which is on the discretion of the embassy.
Tourist and Business visas are generally valid for a stay of maximum 90 days from the first entry, renewable for an equal period at the sole discretion of the Brazilian Federal Police. The maximum stay in Brazil for tourist and business visa holders shall not exceed 180 days from the date of first entry every twelve month.
