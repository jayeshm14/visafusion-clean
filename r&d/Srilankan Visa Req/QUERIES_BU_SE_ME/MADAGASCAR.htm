Consulate General Of Madagascar has representation only in Mumbai

· Passport should be valid for at least 6 months from the date of travel.
· 1 Visa application form duly filled in and signed (photocopy can be used)
· 4 Recent passport size colour photographs with white background.
· Covering letter from the applicant stating his name, designation, purpose and duration of visit.
· Confirmed round trip air tickets

HEALTH REQUIREMENT: Nil

Visa Fee: - Rs. 1,280/- payable by demand draft (D/D) favoring the "Consulate General Of Madagascar"- Mumbai

Time Taken: - Three weeks for processing after receipt of complete application, as documents are referred for visa authorization to Harare. To get this approval these are the following ways:

1.) The counter part arranges this from the Immigration Office - Harare and faxes it directly to the Embassy here.
2.) After the documents have been submitted at the Embassy , the embassy sends the application to the Immigration Office - Harare, and gets the approval

Submission: - Monday to Thursday
Time: - 9:30 am to 1:00pm

Collection: - Monday to Thursday
Time: - 9:30 am to 1:00pm

Note: - Further, any change in fees, additional document or personal presence of the applicant might be required which is on the discretion of the embassy.
