CAPITAL : CANBERRA ( CBR )

WORK VISA

· Passport valid for minimum of 6 months
· 1 visa application form 1066 duly filled in and signed
· 1 form M-67 to be duly filled in with family details and signed
· Form 1196 duly filled in and signed by the prospective employer or sponsor.
· 4 recent passport size colour photographs
· Covering letter stating the applicant's name, address, designation, purpose and duration of stay
· Written offer of employment detailing job title, description of duties to be performed, previous qualifications and experience, length of employment and salary
· Proof of all previous educational and professional qualifications
· "Department of Immigration and Multicultural and Indigenous Affairs" sponsorship / approval from Australia
· Company Bank Statements for the last 6 months
· Company Income Tax Returns for the last 3 years

Health requirements: nil

Fee: Rs.5, 800 /- plus Rs.1, 800 /- payable by two separate demand drafts drawn in favour of "Australian High Commission" - New Delhi And an additional amount of Rs. 450/- payable by cash as a TTS service charge

Time Taken: 5 working days
