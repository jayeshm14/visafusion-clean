CAPITAL : PARIS ( PAR )

FRANCE IS A MEMBER STATE OF THE SCHENGEN AGREEMENT.

THE REGULATIONS STATED BELOW ALSO APPLY TO MONACO.

WORK VISA

· PROCEDURE


The applicant's employer in France has to make an application at the Office des migrations internationales (OMI - Office of Population and Migration) for an employment contract. This organisation is represented in each French department. It will examine the application and get in touch directly with the French consulate under which the applicant come. The consulate in turn will summon the applicant for issuing the visa.

.REQUIREMENTS

· VALID PASSPORT.
· VISA APPLICATION FORM DULY FILLED IN AND SIGNED.
· TWO RECENT PASSPORT SIZE COLOUR PHOTOGRAPHS, 3.5 X 4.5 cm (80% OF THE FACE SHOULD BE VISIBLE WITHOUT BORDER AND TAKEN AGAINST LIGHT BACKGROUND WITH MATT OR SEMI MATT FINISH.
· COVERING LETTER STATING THE APPLICANT'S NAME, DESIGNATION, PURPOSE AND DURATION OF STAY.
· ORIGINAL EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT TO BE ARRANGED BY THE COUNTERPART FRENCH COMPANY AS MENTIONED ABOVE.
· CONFIRMED AIR TICKET.

THE FOLLOWING ADDITIONAL DOCUMENTS ARE REQUIRED FOR FIRST TIME TRAVELLERS :

. BRIEF COMPANY PROFILE
· I/TAX PAPERS FOR LAST THREE YEARS
· BANK STATEMENT FOR LAST THREE MONTHS.

HEALTH REQUIREMENT: MEDICAL INSURANCE REQUIRED FOR STAY DURATION.

FEE: RS. 5, 800 /- VISA PROCESSING FEE. RS. 386/- VFS SERVICE CHARGE (PAYABLE IN CASH) .

TIME TAKEN: 3-4 WORKING DAYS.
