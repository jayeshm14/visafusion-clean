CAPITAL : VIENNA ( VIE )

AUSTRIA IS A MEMBER STATE OF THE “SCHENGEN AGREEMENT”

WORK PERMIT

For a short period stay till 6 months:
· Passport should be valid for the intended stay duration
· Visa Application form duly filled in and signed
· 1 recent passport size colour photograph
· Covering Letter stating the applicant's name, designation, purpose and duration of stay
· Original of police clearance certificate - PCC (English) not older than 90 days
· Original or attested photocopy of birth certificate or school leaving certificate
· Original or attested photocopy of marriage certificate - if applicable
· Invitation letter from the Austrian company/institution confirming the purpose of stay in Austria
· Letter/contract confirming payment of salary, traveling costs, accommodation, etc, for the period of proposed stay in Austria (from Austrian and/or Indian company depending on the arrangement)
· Proof of applicable funds - applicable
· Proof of accommodation: letter from the Austrian company/institution confirming that accommodation has been reserved including address of accommodation.
· Proof of health and accident insurance valid for Austria for the period of proposed stay.
· Original certificate from the local labour office in Austria (ams)
· One photocopy each, of the above mentioned documents strictly on a-4 size only
Please note, for a duration of stay maximum up to 6 months, the temporary residence permit can be issued by the embassy, however application should be made well in advance 9 at least 2-3 weeks) of the proposed travelling date.

For a period stay of more than 6 months:
· Passport should be valid for the intended stay duration
· A copy of all pages of the passport which show entries (copies strictly in a-4 size)
· Visa Application form duly filled in and signed
· 2 recent passport size colour photograph
· Covering Letter stating the applicant's name, designation, purpose and duration of stay
· Original certificate from the local labour office in Austria (ams) and work contract
· Original medical certificate in German language not older than 90 days
· Original of police clearance certificate - PCC (English) not older than 90 days
· Original birth certificate attested by the local authorities and the ministry of external affairs, certified by an Austrian consulate / embassy along with attested German translations thereof.
· Proof of financial support: for employment: employment contract, for business / self-employment: a draft showing that adequate funds have been transferred to an Austrian bank, for private purposes: a sponsorship from a person living in Austria
· Proof of accommodation of the host

Please note, all documents are to be submitted in original along with one set of photocopy of each

Medical Requirement: Health and accident insurance valid for Austria for the period of proposed stay

Time Taken: 2 - 3 weeks

Fee: Rs.5, 050 /- payable in cash

Please note, the Embassy works from Monday to Friday except Wednesdays.

For further details on Austria and its consular procedures, visit http://www.bmaa.gv.at
