CAPITAL : WASHINGTON ( WAS )

WORK VISA

ALL APPLICANTS FOR WORK VISAS MUST HAVE A PETITION APPROVED BY THE "BUREAU OF CITIZENSHIP AND IMMIGRATION SERVICE" - UNITED STATES OF AMERICA (BCIS) BEFORE APPLYING FOR THE VISA.
NOTE: PETITIONS CANNOT BE FILED WITH THE EMBASSY; THEY MUST BE FILED IN THE U.S.
· PASSPORT VALID FOR 6 MONTHS AFTER THE ENTRY INTO UNITED STATES OF AMERICA.
· VISA APPLICATION FORMS - D-157 AND D-158 TO BE DULY FILLED IN AND SIGNED.
· 1 RECENT PASSPORT SIZE (50X50MM AND MEASURING TO A 2 INCH SQUARE. THE HEAD MEASURED FROM THE TOP OF THE HAIR TO THE BOTTOM OF THE CHIN SHOULD MEASURE BETWEEN 28MMX35MM; AND SHOULD BE CLICKED AGAINST A WHITE OR OFF-WHITE BACKGROUND.
· COVERING LETTER STATING THE APPLICANT'S NAME, ADDRESS, CONTACT NUMBER, DESIGNATION, PURPOSE AND DURATION OF STAY.
· I-797 - THE ORIGINAL NOTICE OF APPROVAL.
· I-129 AND THE COMPLETE PETITION SUBMITTED BY YOUR PROSPECTIVE EMPLOYER INCLUDING THE LABOR CONDITION APPLICATION (LCA - NOT APPLICABLE TO L - 1 APPLICANT'S).
· ORIGINALS OF UNIVERSITY DEGREES OR DIPLOMAS ANY OTHER CERTIFICATES PERTAINING TO QUALIFICATION. (SECONDARY SCHOOL INFORMATION NOT REQUIRED)
· WORK EXPERIENCE LETTERS FROM PREVIOUS EMPLOYERS.
· LETTER FROM PETITIONING EMPLOYER CONFIRMING EMPLOYER'S INTENT TO HIRE THE APPLICANT.
· L VISA APPLICANTS MUST PROVIDE A LETTER FROM THEIR EMPLOYER STATING HOW LONG THEY HAVE WORKED FOR THE COMPANY.
· SALARY SLIP FROM CURRENT OR MOST RECENT PLACE OF EMPLOYMENT.
FIRST TIME APPLICANTS ARE ALSO REQUESTED TO BRING THE FOLLOWING ITEMS:
· RECOMMENDATION LETTER ALONG WITH NAMES AND CURRENT PHONE NUMBERS OF THE PERSONNEL MANAGERS AND 2 CO-WORKERS FROM THE APPLICANT'S PRESENT AND PAST JOBS.
· PHOTOGRAPHS OF THE INSIDE AND OUTSIDE OF THE COMPANY'S OFFICES, PROSPECTUS, BROCHURES, AND ANNUAL REPORT OF CURRENT OR MOST RECENT EMPLOYER'S PLACE OF BUSINESS AND ALSO THE SAME FOR THE UNITED STATES COMPANY
· A COMPLETE RESUME AND COVER LETTER DESCRIBING CURRENT JOB DUTIES IN DETAIL.
· PERSONAL BANK RECORDS FOR THE LAST SIX MONTHS.
MEDICAL REQUIREMENT: NIL
FEE:RS. 2, 300 /- NON-REFUNDABLE PROCESSING FEE AND RS.4, 600 /- VISA FEE FOR 6 MONTHS - 10 YEAR MULTIPLE ENTRY APPLICABLE ONLY AFTER THE VISA APPROVAL.
