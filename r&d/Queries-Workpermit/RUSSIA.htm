CAPITAL : MOSCOW ( MOW )

RUSSIAN FEDERATION IS A MEMBER STATE OF THE COMMONWEALTH OF INDEPENDENT STATES.

WORK VISA

· 6 months valid passport along with a photocopy.
· Application form duly filled in and signed.
· 4 recent passport size colour photograph.
· Covering letter stating the applicant's name, designation, purpose and duration of stay.
· Invitation letter from the concerned Russian company should be sent to the Russian ministry, from where approval letter along with the work permit card will be faxed directly to the "Embassy of the Russian Federation" - New Delhi.
· Foreign exchange endorsement on passport.
· Financial support proof - copy of bank statements, copy of credit cards.

Health Requirement: Travel Insurance required for the stay duration, Aids Certificate and School Leaving Certificate

Please note that the above-mentioned certificates should be attested from the "Ministry of External Affairs" - India

Fee: Rs.2, 250 /- for single entry
Rs.4, 500 /- for double entry
Rs.5, 800 /- for multiple entries
Rs.2, 250 /- urgent visa charges i.e. visa within 24 hours.

No fee required for Diplomatic and Official passport holders.

Time taken: All visas are generally issued within 5 - 7 working days from the date of application.
