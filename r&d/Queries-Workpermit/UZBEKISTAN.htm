CAPITAL : TASHKENT ( TAS )

UZBEKISTAN IS A MEMBER STATE OF THE COMMONWEALTH OF INDEPENDENT STATES.

WORK VISA

"THE EMBASSY OF THE REPUBLIC OF UZBEKISTAN" - NEW DELHI DOES NOT ISSUE A WORK VISA. THE COUNTERPART COMPANY IN UZBEKISTAN HAS TO APPLY FOR A WORK PERMIT THROUGH THE "MINISTRY OF FOREIGN AFFAIRS OF UZBEKISTAN" AND A MESSAGE IS DIRECTLY SENT TO THE EMBASSY. AND THE EMBASSY ENACTS AS PER THE MESSAGE INSTRUCTIONS. THE ENTIRE PROCESS IS TIME CONSUMING AND TAKES ABOUT 10 - 15 DAYS AND THE VISA APPLICATION FEE VARIES WITH THE TYPE OF MESSAGE SENT WITH SPECIFICATION ON THE STAY DURATION.
