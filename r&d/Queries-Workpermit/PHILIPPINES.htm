CAPITAL : MANILA ( MNL )

WORK VISA

PROCEDURE
· THE PRINCIPAL COMPANY IN MAILA HAS TO CONTACT THE MANILA BUREAU OF IMMIGRATION FOR THE FORMALITIES.
· THE BUREAU OF IMMIGRATION, MANILA WOULD REFER THE CASE TO THE DEPARTMENT OF FOREIGN AFFAIRS OF MANILA.
· THE DEPARTMENT OF FOREIGN AFFAIRS OF MANILA WOULD BE SENDING THE APPROVAL TO THE EMBASSY DIRECTLY.
· THEN THE APPLICANT CAN APPLY AT PHILIPPINES EMBASSY, NEW DELHI.

REQUIREMENTS

· PASSPORT VALID FOR 6 MONTHS BEYOND THE PROPOSED DURATION
· 2 VISA APPLICATION FORM DULY FILLED IN AND SIGNED.
· 2 RECENT PASSPORT SIZED 2" X 2" COLOUR PHOTOGRAPHS AND THE APPLICANT'S SIGNATURE SHOULD BE THERE ON THE FRONT SIDE.
· COVERING LETTER STATING THE APPLICANT'S NAME, ADDRESS, DESIGNATION, PURPOSE AND DURATION OF STAY. · APPOINTMENT LETTER.
· EMPLOYMENT LETTER, IF APPLICABLE FROM THE CURRENT EMPLOYER INCLUDING THE EMPLOYER'S FULL NAME, ADDRESS AND CONTACT NUMBERS. THE LETTER SHOULD ALSO MENTION THE APPLICANT'S SERVICE DURATION ALONG WITH SALARY DETAILS.
· COPIES OF EDUCATIONAL AND PROFESSIONAL QUALIFICATION CERTIFICATES.
· POLICE CLEARANCE CERTIFICATE.
· CONFIRMED AIR TICKET.

MEDICAL REQUIREMENT: MEDICAL HEALTH CERTIFICATE ( FORM NO.11 ) TO BE ISSUED BY THE EMBASSY AUTHROISED DOCTOR TOGETHER WITH LABORATORY REPORTS OF URINE ( URINE ANALYSIS ) STOOL ( OVAL / PARASITES AND OCCULT BLOOD ) BLOOD ( ROUTINE ) AND CHEST X-RAY PLATES ALONG WITH REPORTS.

TIME TAKEN: APPROX. 3 WORKING DAYS FROM THE DATE OF SUBMISSION (SO LONG COMPLETE REQUIREMENTS ARE SUBMITTED)

NOTE: AFTER THE ISSUANCE OF THE VISA, THE PCC AND THE MEDICAL REPORTS ARE PLACED BY THE EMBASSY INSIDE A PROPERLY SEALED ENVELOPE FOR PRESENTATION AT THE PHILIPPINE IMMIGRATION OFFICER UPON ARRIVAL IN PHILIPPINES.
