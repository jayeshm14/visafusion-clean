CAPITAL : NICOSIA ( NIC )

WORK VISA

. THE HIGH COMMISSION OF THE REPUBLIC OF CYPRUS DOES NOT ISSUE A WORK VISA.TO APPLY FOR A WORK VISA THE FOLLOWING PROCEDURES ARE INVOLED:

· THE COUNTERPART COMPANY IN CYPRUS APPLIES FOR A WORK PERMIT AT THE IMMIGRATION DEPARTMENT OF THE MINISTRY OF INTERIORS - CYPRUS.
· AN APPROVAL FAX LETTER MENTIONING ALL DETAILS OF THE APPLICANT IS SEND TO HIM AND THE ORIGINAL PERMIT REMAINS WITH THE IMMIGRATION DEPARTMENT OF CYPRUS.
· APPLICANT NEEDS TO TRAVEL WITH THE FAX LETTER.
· ON REACHING THE PORT, THE FAX LETTER WILL BE CROSS CHECKED WITH THE ORIGINAL PERMIT COPY AND HE WILL GET HIS VISA STAMPED.
