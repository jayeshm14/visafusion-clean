CAPITAL : MADRID ( MAD )

SPAIN IS A MEMBER STATE OF THE SCHENGEN AGREEMENT.

WORK VISA

· Passport should be valid for 6 months
· 4 Visa Application forms duly filled in and signed.
· 4 Recent passport size colour photographs
· Covering Letter mentioning the applicant's name, designation, purpose and duration of stay.
· Work permit to be arranged by the counterpart Spanish company and sent to the applicant.
· Educational and professional certificates.
· Financial solvency proof - bank statements, I / tax return papers, copy of credit cards.
· Confirmed one-way air ticket.

Note: for further information and to apply for a work visa applicant has to personally contact "The Embassy of Spain" - New Delhi with all related documents.
