WORK VISA

"THE EMBASSY OF THE STATE OF KUWAIT" - NEW DELHI DOES NOT ISSUE A WORK VISA.
· A "NO OBJECTION CERTIFICATE" TO ENTER THE COUNTRY HAS TO BE ARRANGED BY THE COUNTERPART INVITING COMPANY IN KUWAIT.
· AN APPROVAL LETTER ALONG WITH A "NO OBJECTION CERTIFICATE" IS ISSUED BY THE MINISTRY OF LABOUR OF KUWAIT IN REFERENCE TO THE ABOVE IS SENT TO THE APPLICANT.
· UPON RECEIVING THE ABOVE MENTIONED DOCUMENTS, APPLICANT HAS TO PERSONALLY CONTACT "THE EMBASSY OF THE STATE OF KUWAIT" - NEW DELHI, FOR THE ENDORSEMENT OF THE MENTIONED DOCUMENTS.

MEDICAL REQUIREMENT: MEDICAL TEST AS PER THE DIRECTIONS FROM THE EMBASSY.

FEE: RS. 3,000 /-

TIME TAKEN: 3 - 4 WORKING DAYS.
