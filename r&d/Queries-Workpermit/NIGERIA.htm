CAPITAL : ABUJA ( ABV )

WORK VISA / STR VISA

. Passport valid for 6 months along with photocopies of 1st, 2nd and last page.
. 2 Visa application form duly filled in and signed.
. 4 recent passport size colour photographs.
. Covering Letter stating the applicant's name, address, designation, purpose and duration of the stay
. Original and photocopies of appointment letter and contract agreement between the applicant and the government
. Original Educational and professional certificates along with photocopies
. Copy of the approved cable for temporary employment from the 'Nigerian Immigration Service' and duly attested from the 'Ministry of Foreign Affairs' - Nigeria.
. CV or Credentials of the applicant
. Board of Director's Resolution
. Foreign Exchange Endorsement / Copy of Credit Cards
. Confirmed Return / Onward Air Ticket.
. Security Clearance of the application

Medical Requirement: Yellow Fever Certificate required for travel.

Fee: Rs.5, 500 /- for TWP & STR Visa
Rs.8, 500 /- for Work Visa
All fee payable by either cash or demand draft drawn in favour of 'Nigerian High Commission' - New Delhi.

Please note, Double visa fee in case of an urgent visa.

THE APPLICATION HAS TO BE SUBMITTED THROUGH THE EMBASSY AUTHORIZED CENTER VISWANATH - VISA FACILITATION CENTER ;THE SERVICE CHARGES OF THE CENTER IS RS. 850/- INCLUSIVE OF ALL TAXES.

PLEASE NOTE: ALL FEES ARE NON REFUNDABLE IRRESPECTIVE OF THE VISA GRANTED OR NOT.

Time Taken: 48 hours for Normal Visa
Same day for Urgent visa
