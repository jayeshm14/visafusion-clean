WORK VISA

PROCEDURE

· APPLICANT HAS TO GET HIS / HER BIRTH CERTIFICATE LEGALISED FROM THE 'LEGALISATION DEPARTMENT' AT THE "ROYAL NETHERLANDS EMBASSY" - NEW DELHI.
· A MVV APPLICATION THROUGH APPOINTMENT SYSTEM IS REQUIRED TO ENTER NETHERLANDS AND OBTAIN A RESIDENCE PERMIT TO STAY FOR A PERIOD LONGER THAN 3 MONTHS.
· A MVV APPLICATION CAN ONLY BE INITIATED ON THE BASIS OF A VERIFIED AND LEGALISED BIRTH-CERTIFICATE BY THE EMBASSY OR CONSULATE-GENERAL OF EVERY APPLICANT.
· AFTER THE MVV HAS BEEN ISSUED, IT IS ONLY VALID FOR A PERIOD OF THREE DAYS AFTER ENTERING THE NETHERLANDS. THE HOLDER OF THE MVV HAS TO REPORT TO THE ALIEN POLICE WITHIN A PERIOD OF THREE DAYS AFTER ARRIVING IN THE NETHERLANDS TO APPLY FOR A RESIDENCE PERMIT (VTV).

REQUIREMENTS

· PASSPORT VALID FOR ATLEAST 6 MONTHS AFTER INTENDED DATE OF TRAVEL.
· VISA APPLICATION FORM DULY FILLED IN AND SIGNED.
· 2 RECENT PASSPORT SIZE COLOUR PHOTOGRAPHS.
· COVERING LETTER STATING THE APPLICANT'S NAME, DESIGNATION, PURPOSE AND DURATION OF STAY.
· A VERIFIED AND LEGALISED BIRTH CERTIFICATE.
· WORK PERMIT TO BE ARRANGED BY THE COUNTERPART COMPANY IN NETHERLANDS AND SENT TO THE APPLICANT.
· APPOINTMENT LETTER / WORK CONTRACT LETTER.
· PROOF OF EDUCATIONAL AND PROFESSIONAL CERTIFICATES.
· FINANCIAL SOLVENCY PROOF - BANK STATEMENTS, COPIES OF CREDIT CARDS, I/TAX RETURNS. (NOT MANDATORY)
· CONFIRMED AIR TICKET.

MEDICAL REQUIREMENT: MEDICAL INSURANCE FOR THE STAY DURATION.

FEE: APPROXIMATELY RS.2, 700 /- PAYABLE BY DEMAND DRAFT DRAWN IN FAVOUR OF "THE ROYAL NETHERLANDS EMBASSY" - NEW DELHI.

TIME TAKEN: 8 - 10 WEEKS AS THE APPLICATIONS ARE SENT TO "THE IMMIGRATION DEPARTMENT UNDER THE MINISTRY OF JUSTICE OF NETHERLANDS" FOR APPROVAL.

NOTE: · ALL DOCUMENTS HAVE TO BE SUBMITTED IN ORIGINAL.
· ALL DOCUMENTS HAVE TO BE ATTESTED BY THE COMPETENT INDIAN AUTHORITIES.
· IF ANY DOCUMENT IS IN A LOCAL LANGUAGE, AN OFFICIAL NOTARIZED TRANSLATION INTO ENGLISH HAS TO BE SUBMITTED ALONG WITH IT.
· IF ANY DOCUMENT IS ISSUED IN RAJASTHAN, ORISSA OR A STATE FURTHUR TO THE NORTH, AN ADDITIONAL ATTESTATION BY THE MINISTRY OF EXTERNAL AFFAIRS OF INDIA HAS TO BE OBTAINED. AND IF ISSUED FROM THE STATE OF PUNJAB OTHER THAN CHANDIGARH, IN THAT CASE IT HAS TO BE ATTESTED BY THE DEPUTY COMMISIONER OF CHANDIGARH AND THE INDIAN OVERSEAS CELL OF CHANDIGARH ALSO.
