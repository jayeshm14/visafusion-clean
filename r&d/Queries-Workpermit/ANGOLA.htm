CAPITAL : ANGOLA

WORK

· Passport valid along with photocopy of the main pages of those which contain information of international travel.
· Visa Application form duly filled and signed
· 2 recent passport size color photographs
· Covering Letter stating the applicant's name, designation, purpose and duration of stay
· Medical Certificate duly attested.
· Certificate of Educational & Professional qualifications.
A. For the purpose of employment in companies such as diamond companies, state owned companies, private companies, mixed associations & co-operatives:
· Application of the contracting entity
· Police Clearance Certificate or document from the police authorities from the area of the concerned person, which declares that there is no criminal record as per the law of the country of the applicant.
· Declaration saying that he promises to respect the Angolan laws
· Individual contract of work, in duplicate.
· Letter from the Ministry of Tutela with a favorable opinion for the issue of the employment visa.
B. For the purpose of employment with the petroleum companies:
· Letter from the Ministry of Petroleum with a favorable opinion for the issue of the work visa.
· Certificate of Educational & Professional qualifications.
C. For the purpose of employment with the entities involved in agreements with government, bi-lateral and non -governmental organizations:
· Application of the contracting entity.
· Letter from the Ministry of Tutela with a favorable opinion for the issue of work visa.
· Declaration saying that he promises to respect the Angolan laws
· Police Clearance Certificate or a letter from the from the police authorities from the area of the concerned persons saying that there is no criminal record as per the laws of the country of the applicant
· Individual work contract, in duplicate
N.B. When the contracting entity requires a work visa for a foreign citizen for the first time, they must enclose the copy of the agreement signed between them and the Government of Republic of Angola that legalizes its activity
When the applicant entities (companies) request for the first time, a visa for its foreign workers, they must enclose a legal proof of its juridical personality, such as a copy of the social pact of the charter of an authorized economic activity and a proof of payment of duties to the ex-chequer
The Notary and Ministry of External Affairs of the country of the applicant must attest the document pertaining to the Point No.1 & 2 of the present notice.

The professional qualification certificates should be translated into Portuguese.
Medical Requirement: Medical Certificate duly attested.
Fee: Rs.6, 000 /- payable by cash
Time Taken: Approx. 15 days, as application is send to Angola for approval
