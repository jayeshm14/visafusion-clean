WORK VISA

"THE EMBASSY OF PANAMA" - NEW DELHI DOES NOT ISSUE A WORK VISA. THE APPLICANT HAS TO ADVICE THE ASSOCIATE COMPANY IN PANAMA HAS TO CONTACT THE IMMIGRATION DEPARTMENT OF PANAMA TO APPLY FOR THE WORK PERMIT AND SENT IT TO THE APPLICANT. UPON RECEIVING MENTIONED DOCUMENTS, APPLICANT HAS TO PERSONALLY CONTACT THE "THE EMBASSY OF PANAMA" - NEW DELHI FOR ENDORSEMENT ON THE PASSPORT.
