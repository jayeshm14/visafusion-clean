CAPITAL : OTTAWA ( YOW )

WORK VISA

· Valid passport.
· Work visa application form duly filled and signed.
· 3 recent passport size colour photographs."Please note that the applicant should mention his / her name and date of birth at the back of each photograph enclosed"
· Covering letter stating the applicant's name, address, contact number, designation, purpose and duration of visit,
· HRDC approval letter or invitation letter from the canadian company.
· Appointment letter.
· Salary slip of the last / present working company.
· NOC from the present employer.
· Certified photocopies of all educational and professional qualifications.
· Company profile.
· Financial solvency proof - income tax papers, etc.

Fee: Rs.5, 550/-- for multiple entries
All fee payable by demand draft drawn in favour of "Canadian High Commission" - New Delhi.

VFS Service charge of Rs. 505/- payable by cash

Time taken: 24 hours.

Note: Personal presence of the applicant is mandatory for first time traveler. But this is not applied in case of applicant who is a software engineer.

Please note: With effect from March 7th 2005, all visa applications for Canada has to be submitted and collected through 'Visa Facilitation Services' (VFS). The submission timings at the VFS center are from 08:00 - 12:00 am in the morning and 13:00 - 16:00 pm in the afternoon.
