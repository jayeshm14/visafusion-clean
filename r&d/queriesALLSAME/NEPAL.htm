CAPITAL : KATHMANDU ( KTM )

NATIONALS OF INDIA DO NOT REQUIRE A VISA TO TRAVEL TO NEPAL.

INDIAN NATIONALS MUST HOLD PROOF OF IDENTITY ISSUED BY COMPETENT INDIAN AUTHORITIES TO THE EFFECT THAT THEY ARE RESIDING IN INDIA AND ARE NATIONALS OF INDIA.

SUCH PROOF CAN BE IN THE FORM OF INDIAN PASSPORT, THE ELECTION CARD.

ALL TOURIST VISAS ARE VALID FOR TRAVEL TO KATHMANDU VALLEY, POKHARA VALLEY, TIGER TOPS IN CHITWAN. HOWEVER, IF VISITING OTHER PLACES OR TREKKING IN NEPAL IS INVOLVED THAN PERMITS CAN BE OBTAINED FROM THE CENTRAL IMMIGRATION OFFICE IN KATHMANDU.
