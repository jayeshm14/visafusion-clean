CAPITAL : THIMPU ( QJC )

VISA REQUIRED.

ISSUED BY THE DEPARTMENT OF TOURISM IN BHUTAN. ALL VISITORS ARE REQUIRED TO BOOK WITH A REGISTERED TOUR OPERATOR IN BHUTAN. VISITORS TO HOLD CONFIRMED RETURN / ONWARD AIR TICKET, SUFFICIENT FUNDS FOR STAY DURATION, TWO PHOTOGRAPHS.

PASSENGERS MUST OBTAIN A “VISA CLEARANCE” THROUGH THE TOUR OPERATOR , WHICH TAKES ABOUT 10 DAYS TO PROCESS. VISA WILL BE STAMPED IN PASSPORT AT THE PORT OF ENTRY UPON PAYMENT OF USD 20. VISA IS VALID FOR 15 DAYS. EXTENSION OF VISA UPTO SIX MONTHS CAN BE OBTAINED AT THE MINISTRY OF FOREIGN AFFAIRS (VISA DIVISION) – THIMPU.
