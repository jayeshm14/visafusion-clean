CAPITAL : CASTRIES ( SLU )

INDEPENDENT ST. LUCIA IS ONE OF THE WINDWARD ISLANDS WHICH FORM THE SOUTHERN PART OF THE LESSER ANTILLES THAT STRETCH IN A SEMI CIRCLE FROM VENEZUELA TO PUERTO RICO.

NO VISA REQUIRED FOR INDIAN NATIONALS FOR STAY UPTO 28 DAYS, AFTER WHICH EXTENSIONS MAY BE REQUESTED FROM THE POLICE DEPARTMENT.
