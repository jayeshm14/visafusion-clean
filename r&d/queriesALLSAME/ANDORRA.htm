CAPITAL : ANDORRA ( ALV )

ANDORRA IS AN INDEPENDENT COUNTRY SITUATED BETWEEN FRANCE AND SPAIN.

NO DIPLOMATIC REPRESENTATION IN INDIA, VISA REQUIRED.

ANDORRA ACCEPTS ANY PASSPORT OR PASSPORT REPLACING DOCUMENTS VALID FOR EITHER FRANCE OR SPAIN.

NO SPECIFIC VISA REQUIREMENTS EXIST FOR ANDORRA. HOWEVER, ENTRY REGULATIONS OF FRANCE OR SPAIN ARE APPLICABLE – SUBJECT TO WHICHEVER POINT IS TRANSITED TO REACH ANDORRA.
