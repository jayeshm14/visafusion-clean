CAPITAL : PAGO PAGO ( PPG )

THESE ISLANDS ARE SITUATED IN THE PACIFIC OCEAN ALONG THE INTERNATIONAL DATE LINE AND NORTH OF TONGA. AMERICAN SAMOA HAS ENTRY REGULATIONS OF ITS OWN. PAGO PAGO IS NOT A PORT OF ENTRY TO THE UNITED STATES AND PASSENGERS INTENDING TO PROCEED TO THE UNITED STATES, MUST COMPLY WITH THE APPROPRIATE REGULATIONS.

VISA REQUIRED, ASSOCIATES TO CONTACT THE OFFICE OF THE GOVERNOR OR ATTORNEY GENERAL IN PAGO PAGO, TO FORWARD THE VISA AUTHORIZATION TO TRAVEL.
