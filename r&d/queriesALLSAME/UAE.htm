CAPITAL : ABU DHABI ( AUH )

THE UNITED ARAB EMIRATES COMPRISE THE FOLLOWING INDEPENDENT SHEIKHDOMS : ABU DHABI, AJMAN, DUBAI, FUJAIRAH, RAS AL KHAIMAH, SHARJAH AND UMM AL QWAIN.

A 14 DAYS TRANSIT VISA CAN BE ISSUED UPON ARRIVAL BY THE IMMIGRATION AUTHORITIES, PROVIDED PASSENGER HOLDING A COPY OF THE VISA MESSAGE. THIS VISA MUST BE USED WITHIN 30 DAYS AFTER DATE OF ISSUANCE. THIS VISA CAN BE USED TO ENTER AND LEAVE THE UNITED ARAB FROM ANY AIRPORT. THE SPONSOR MUST MEET THE PASSENGER AT THE AIRPORT WITH THE ORIGINAL VISA CLEARANCE.

THE SPONSOR ( A COMPANY / HOTEL / INDIVIDUAL PERSON ) TO APPLY WITH PASSENGER’S PASSPORT & FLIGHT DETAILS FOR A VISA CLEARANCE WITH THE IMMIGRATION AUTHORITIES. IT TAKES AT LEAST THREE WORKING DAYS AFTER RECEIVING APPLICATION TO PROCESS THE VISA.
