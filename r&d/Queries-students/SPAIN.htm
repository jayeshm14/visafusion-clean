CAPITAL : MADRID ( MAD )

SPAIN IS A MEMBER STATE OF THE SCHENGEN AGREEMENT.

STUDENT VISA (for courses longer than 3 months)

· Passport should be valid for 6 months
· 4 Visa Application forms duly filled in and signed.
· 4 Recent passport size colour photographs
· Covering Letter mentioning the applicant's name, designation, purpose and duration of stay.
· Original invitation letter from the university in Spain specifying the duration of the course (the tuition time of which should be at least 15hrs/week) acceptance of inscription and payment of the fees.
· All previous educational and professional certificates (if any)
· Financial solvency proof of either the applicant or the sponsor (if any) - bank statements, I / tax return papers, copy of credit cards.
· Police Clearance Certificate should be not older than 3 months. Please Note: In case of Indians, it should be from the "Passport office"
· For minors less than 18 years old, a written permission, attested by the competent authorities, should authorize the minor to travel and study in Spain.
· Confirmed one-way air ticket.

Medical Requirement: Medical certificate on the letter-head of a authorized MBBS doctor or from a hospital should state that the applicant does not suffer from any mental or contagious disease; and the medical certificate must not be older than 3 months.

Fee: Rs.3, 132 /- payable by cash (non-refundable)

Time Taken: Approx 7 - 10 working days after approval from Madrid.

Note:
. It is necessary to add to the original documents two sets of photocopies of the required documents.
· Once the visa has been approved by the "Ministry of External Affairs" - Spain, there is a limited period of 2 months to collect the same from the embassy. Failure to do so from the date of the approval of the visa will result in the cancellation of the same. the visa, if granted, must be collected personally by the applicant or in the case of minors, by the parents or guardians.
