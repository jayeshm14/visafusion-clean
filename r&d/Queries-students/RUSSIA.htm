CAPITAL : MOSCOW ( MOW )

RUSSIAN FEDERATION IS A MEMBER STATE OF THE COMMONWEALTH OF INDEPENDENT STATES.

STUDENT VISA

. Passport valid for 6 months
. Visa Application form duly filled in and signed.
. 4 Recent passport size colour photographs.
. Covering Letter stating the applicant's name, address, designation, purpose and duration of stay.
. All documents pertaining to the admission to the university / institution and also the course details.
. All educational and professional certificates.
. Confirmed Air ticket.

Health Requirement: Travel Insurance required for the stay duration, Aids Certificate and School Leaving Certificate

Please note that the above-mentioned certificates should be attested from the "Ministry of External Affairs" - India

Fee: Rs.2, 250 /- for single entry
Rs.4, 500 /- for double entry
Rs.5, 800 /- for multiple entries
Rs.2, 250 /- urgent visa charges i.e. visa within 24 hours.

No fee required for Diplomatic and Official passport holders.

Time: 3 working days.

Please note:
1) Personal Presence of the applicant mandatory
2) Please note that the Embassy is closed on all Wednesdays.
3) The validity of the visa issued will be effective after 5 working days from the date of issue.
