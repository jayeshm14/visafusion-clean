CAPITAL : OTTAWA ( YOW )

STUDENT VISA

· Valid passport.
· Attested copies of the passport.
· Student visa application form duly filled and signed.
· 3 recent passport size colour photographs."Please note that the applicant should mention his / her name and date of birth at the back of each photograph enclosed"
· Covering letter stating the applicant's name, address, contact number, designation, purpose and duration of visit.
· Admission / Offer Letter of course from the Canadian University / Institution.
· TOEFL scorecard.
· Original Educational Certificates along with attested photocopy of all the certificates - School Leaving, Higher Secondary (10+2), Graduation, Post-Graduation, etc.
· Original Work experience Certificates along with attested photocopy of all certificates
· Reference Letters from all previous employers and also from the present employer
· Salary slip of the last / present working company.
· NOC from the present employer.
· Statement of purpose from the applicant.
· Company profile.
· Financial solvency documents:
In case of self finance:
a. Parent / Guardian personal bank statement for the last 3 months
In case of sponsor:
a. Bond signed between sponsor and applicant
b. Sponsor's bank statement for the last 3 months
c. Income Tax papers for the last 3 years
d. Affidavit of support from sponsor
In case of bank loan:
a. Bank Statement for the last 3 months from parent / guardian
b. Income Tax Papers for the last 3 years
c. Affidavit of support and consent from parents
d. Letter from bank

Medical Requirement: Medical Clearance requirement depends on the Embassy discretion.

Fee: Rs.4, 625/-- for multiple entries
All fee payable by demand draft drawn in favour of "Canadian High Commission" - New Delhi.
VFS Service charge of Rs. 505/- payable by cash

Time taken: 24 hours.

Note: Personal presence of the applicant is mandatory for first time traveler. But this is not applied in case of applicant who is a software engineer.

Please note: With effect from March 7th 2005, all visa applications for Canada has to be submitted and collected through 'Visa Facilitation Services' (VFS). The submission timings at the VFS center are from 08:00 - 12:00 am in the morning and 13:00 - 16:00 pm in the afternoon.
