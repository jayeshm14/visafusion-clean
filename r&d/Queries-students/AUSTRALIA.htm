CAPITAL : CANBERRA ( CBR )

STUDENT VISA

· Passport valid for minimum of 6 months
· 1 visa application Form 48 (R) duly filled in and signed
· 1 Form M-67 to be duly filled with family details and signed
· 1 recent passport size colour photograph
· Covering letter on the business letterhead stating the applicant's name, address, designation, purpose and duration of stay
· Letter from the principal of the school detailing purpose, duration, names of student/s and teacher/s who would be going for the exchange program
· Bona fide certificate from the school for each applicant
· No objection certificate from parents of children either with their passport copies or duly attested from notary
· Invitation letter from Australian counterpart should specify the dates and the duration of the exchange program, mentioning all the names of all the students and teachers
· Evidence of funds - company Bank Statements for the last 6 months and Company Income Tax Returns for the last 3 years

Please note. if the applicant is applying for a period more than 4 weeks in that case the consular will have to assess all the documents and advice accordingly

Health Requirements: Medicals required if the applicant is going for more than 4 weeks

Fee: Rs.2, 200 /- payable by demand draft drawn in favour of "Australian High Commission" - New Delhi And an additional amount of Rs. 450/- payable by cash as a TTS service charge

Time Taken: 5 working days
