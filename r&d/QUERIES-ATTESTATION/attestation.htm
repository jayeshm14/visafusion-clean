ATTESTATIONS

MARRIAGE AND BIRTH CERTIFICATES

CERTIFICATES TO BE FIRST ATTESTED BY THE ‘UNDER SECRETARY TO THE HOME DEPARTMENT’ OF THE STATE GOVERNMENT IN THE STATE OF ISSUE OF THE DOCUMENT.

THE CERTIFICATES SHOULD THEN BE FORWARDED TO US FOR ATTESTATION FROM THE “MINISTRY OF EXTERNAL AFFAIRS” FOLLOWED BY THE ATTESTATION FROM THE CONCERNED EMBASSY.


EDUCATIONAL CERTIFICATES

CERTIFICATES SHALL BE FIRST SUBMITTED FOR ATTESTATION BY THE ‘MINISTRY OF EDUCATION” FOLLOWED BY ATTESTATION BY THE “MINISTRY OF EXTERNAL AFFAIRS”

AND FINALLY SUBMITTED FOR THE ATTESTATION BY THE CONCERNED EMBASSY.

SUPPORTING DOCUMENTS TO BE ENCLOSED

  • ONE RECENT PASSPORT SIZE PHOTOGRAPH
  • PHOTOCOPY OF THE PASSPORTS
  • ALL OTHER MARKSHEETS IN ORIGINAL FOR REFERENCE


BUSINESS DOCUMENTS

DOCUMENTS TO BE FIRST ATTESTED BY THE “CHAMBER OF COMMERCE” OF THE CONCERNED STAFF AND FORWARDED TO US FOR ATTESTATION BY THE “MINISTRY OF EXTERNAL AFFAIRS” FOLLOWED BY THE ATTESTATION BY THE CONCERNED EMBASSY.


TIME TAKEN : 7 DAYS

THE DEGREES ISSUED AT HYDERABAD AND ANDHRA PRADESH SHOULD BE ATTESTED BY THE GENERAL ADMINISTRATION DEPARTMENT – HYDERABAD.

DEGREES ISSUED BY THE DHARWAD UNIVERSITY – KARNATAKA: THE REGISTRAR OF THE DHARWAD UNIVERSITY SHOULD FIRST ATTEST A PHOTOCOPY OF THE DEGREE.
