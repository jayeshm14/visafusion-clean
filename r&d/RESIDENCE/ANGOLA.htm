CAPITAL : ANGOLA

RESIDENCE VISA

· Valid Passport.
. Visa Application Form to be filled in original and a duplicate.
· 2 Recent coloured photographs. ·
. Photocopy of the passport and of the main pages and of those which contain information about international travel.
· Medical Certificate duly attested.
· Police Clearance Certificate which declares that the concerned person has no criminal record as per the law of the country of the applicant.
· Affidavit certified by Notary of the beneficiary promising to respect the Angolan law.
· Proof of funds for subsistence in Angola.
· Proof of existence of relations with the nationals or foreigners residing in Angola.

HEALTH REQUIREMENT : Medical Certificate duly attested

FEE : Rs. 6, 000/- Payable by cash. Fee required after the approval of the visa.

TIME TAKEN : Approx. 15 days, as application is send to Angola for approval.
