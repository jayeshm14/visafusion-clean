"THE EMBASSY OF THE STATE OF KUWAIT" - NEW DELHI DOES NOT ISSUE A TOURIST VISA. A VISA TO ENTER THE COUNTRY IS ARRANGED BY THE COUNTERPART INVITING PARTY / SPONSOR IN KUWAIT AND AN APPROVAL LETTER ALONG WITH THE "NO OBJECTION CERTIFICATE" IN REFERENCE TO THE ABOVE IS SENT TO THE APPLICANT. APPLICANT TRAVELS WITH THIS LETTER AND GETS VISA ON ARRIVAL AT THE PORT.
