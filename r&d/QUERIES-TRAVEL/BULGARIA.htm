CAPITAL : SOFIA ( SOF )

TOURIST

· PASSPORT SHOULD BE VALID FOR ATLEAST 3 MONTHS AFTER THE DATE OF EXIT FROM BULGARIA.
· VISA APPLICATION FORM DULY FILLED IN AND SIGNED.
· 1 RECENT PASSPORT SIZE COLOUR PHOTOGRAPH.
· ORIGINAL COVERING LETTER ON THE COMPANY LETTER HEAD STATING THE APPLICANT'S NAME, DESIGNATION, PURPOSE AND DURATION OF VISIT.
· ORIGINAL DOCUMENT OR VOUCHER ISSUED BY A TOURIST ORGANIZATION OR TOUR OPERATOR FOR PRE-PAID TOURIST SERVICES. THE VOUCHER SHOULD EXPLICITLY CONTAIN THE LICENSE NUMBER OF THE TOURIST ORGANISATION OR TOUR OPERATOR, ISSUED BY THE BULGARIAN MINISTRY OF ECONOMY, THE NAME AND LICENSE NUMBER OF THE HOTEL AND THE DURATION OF THE PRE-PAID TOURIST SERVICES.
· CONFIRMED RETURN AIR TICKET.
· FOREIGN EXCHANGE ENDORSEMENT ON PASSPORT/COPY OF CREDIT CARD.

MEDICAL REQUIREMENT: MEDICAL INSURANCE FOR STAY DURATION. FOR PASSENGERS STAYING IN BULGARIA FOR A PERIOD LONGER THAN 30 DAYS, HAVE TO SUBMIT MEDICAL TEST FOR HIV/AIDS VIRUS CERTIFIED BY THE MINISTRY OF EXTERNAL AFFAIRS OF INDIA.
