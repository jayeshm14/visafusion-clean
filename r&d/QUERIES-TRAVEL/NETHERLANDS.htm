NOW NETHERLANDS EMBASSY WILL ONLY ACCEPT VISAS AND MVV APPLICATIONS THROUGH AN APPOINTMENT SYSTEM.

HOW TO MAKE YOUR APPOINTMENT:- YOU CALL THE NETHERLANDS EMBASSY THROUGH A SPECIAL NO. FOR APPOINTMENT FOR VISA:-(011)-55687000.

APPOINETMENT CAN BE MADE ON MONDAYS THROUGH THURSDAYS BETWEEN 10:00 - 14:00 HOURS.
WHILE MAKING THE APPOINTMENT YOU HAVE TO PROVIDE THE OFFICER WITH THE FOLLOWING DETAILS:- 1.) DATE OF BIRTH OF APPLICANT. 2.) NAME OF THE APPLICANT. 3.) PASSPORT NO. OF THE APPLICANT.

FOR THE APPOINTMENT YOU HAVE THREE OPTIONS:-
1.) BETWEEN 09:00 -10:00 A.M 2.) BETWEEN 10:00 - 11:00 A.M 3.) BETWEEN 11:00 - 12:00 A.M IT IS ESSENTIAL THAT YOU COME IN TIME ACCORDING TO THE FIXED APPOINTMENT.

2.) DO NOT COME LATE BECAUSE IF YOU DO SO, YOUR APPOINTMENT WILL BE CANCELLED AND YOU HAVE TO CALL THE EMBASSY AGAIN IN ORDER TO MAKE A NEW APPOINTMENT.

3.) ALL PERSONS WHO COME TO THE VISA SECTION WITHOUT A FIXED APPOINTMENT WILL NOT GAIN ACCESS TO THE VISA SECTION OF THE EMBASSY.APPOINTMENT CAN NOT BE ARRANGED AT THE GATE, BUT ONLY BY TELEPHONE.
