CAPITAL : ARMENIA

TOURIST

· Passport valid for minimum of 6 months
· Visa Application form duly filled in and signed
· 1 recent passport size colour photograph
· Covering letter mentioning the applicant's name, designation, duration and purpose of stay
· Hotel confirmation
· Confirmed air tickets
· Financial support proof - Bank Statements, copies of Credit Cards, Income Tax Returns

Medical Requirement: Nil

Fee: Rs.1, 750 /- for single entry
Rs.3, 250 /- for multiple entry
Please note, US $ 90 for urgent cases (may vary with applications)

Time Taken: 7 working days from the date of submission.
