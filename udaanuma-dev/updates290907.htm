Attention All Applicants Traveling To Argentina...


With immediate effect all applicants traveling to Argentina on a Business Visit will now have tosubmit the Original covering letter duly attested by the local NOTARY PUBLIC. The invite letter should also be in Original & be attested by the local NOTARY PUBLIC in Argentina.

Applicants traveling for a Private Visit are required to submit the Original Sponsorship letter, Passport Valid Resident Copy & DNI Copy.

An Hotel Voucher & a Day to Day Iternary has to be submitted along with the other documents by the travellers applying for a Tourist Visa
