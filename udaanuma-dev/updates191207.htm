19 December 2007

  
 



Dear Travel Partners,

Update from Hungary Embassy:-

This is to update you that Hungary Embassy will not accept visa applications on 20th December. They will start accepting visa applications with the new schengen visa form w.e.f 27th December. You can download the new visa form from our website.

Just for your information - There is no submissions on Friday's and embassy will be closed from 24-26 December.
