18 December 2006

  
 


HIGH COMMISSION OF THE FEDERAL REPUBLIC OF NIGERIA :


Relief, No more additional charges
The Nigerian High Commission has discontinued its visa process outsourcing with its authorized agent. Therefore, the additional fee of Rs 850/- has been waived off but the official visa fee remains the same.

Udaan India Private Limited can now make submissions directly to the embassy thus making the Visa procedure hassle free.



ROYAL EMBASSY OF BELGIUM:

This is to inform that the Embassy of Belgium will be closed from 22nd December to 2nd January 2007, therefore no submissions or collection would take place.



EMBASSY OF PHILIPPINES :

The " Embassy of Philippines " will be closed from 25th December to 2nd January, 2007. Hence there would be no submissions on the above mentioned dates.
All applications for visa processing submitted on 21st December can be collected on 27th December in the afternoon.
The submissions made on 22nd December can be collected on 28th December between 10am-11 am.


BRITISH HIGH COMMISSION :

The High Commission will be closed from 25th to 27th December 2006. All applications for visa processing submitted on 22nd December can be collected on 28th December in the evening.

Please note that there will be no interview scheduled between 23rd December 2006 to 2nd January 2007.
