Special Visa For Cricket World Cup-2007 (CARICOM)


The " Embassy of Trinidad & Tobago " -New Delhi will be issuing special visa for the World Cup 2007 from 20th December 2006. This would be issued by the Caribbean community (CARICOM) and shall be valid from 15th January to 15th May 2007. Any visitor traveling on this visa would be able to move freely in the below mentioned countries during his/her stay.



Antigua & Barbuda Barbados
Dominica Grenada
Guyana Jamaica
St. Kitts & Nevis Saint Lucia
St. Vincent & The Grenadines Trinidad & Tobago



Apart from the Indian travelers, Tourist traveling from Bangladesh, Pakistan and Srilanka will have to take the CARICOM special visa from " Embassy of Trinidad & Tobago "-New Delhi, India
