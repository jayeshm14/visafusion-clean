Dear Travel Partners,

Switzerland Visa Application for Honeymoon Couples:

In case if a honeymoon couple is a 1st time traveller or have not travelled to countries like Schengen, UK, US, Australia then Swiss embassy (VFS) is not accepting their visa applications before the couples marriage. Visa application has to be lodged only after their marriage and with appropriate proof of marriage. In all probability they will be called for an interview. Kindly keep your passengers updated on this requirement.

Spain Embassy - Requirement of Photocopies of all passport pages including old passports.

Spain Embassy (IVS) is asking for Photocopies of all passport pages including old passports at the time of submission. Kindly forward all photocopies along with visa application to avoid any delay in submission.

  China Embassy -

  Inspite of our regular updates, we are still receiving old visa forms. We have beenchanging the visa forms in our office but to avoid any delay in visa submission kindly download new visa forms from our website and send the same along with your documents.
