New entrants in Schengen World:-

Dear Travel Partners,

 This is just an udpate to apprise you on the new developments taking place for availing Schengen visa. Although there is no circular from respective embassies supporting this information but we have come across this information from various sources.

Given below is information from website www.schengenspace.com

The European Union's ten new members (namely - Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, Poland, Slovakia, Hungary, the Czech Republic, Slovenia, Malta and Cyprus) will join the borderless Schengen zone in 2007, according to the Justice and Interior ministers from the European Union.

These members must use Schengen standards at customs and border points in order to join the other fifteen members (namely Austria ,Germany, Belgium, Denmark, Finland, France, Greece, Iceland, Italy, Luxemburg, Norway, Portugal, Spain, Sweden, The Netherlands). Those who meet the criteria will be able to integrate into Schengen system in 2007. Land borders are scheduled to be opened in December 31, 2007 and sea and air borders checks will be lifted by latest March 2008. In mid-2007 the European Commission will evaluate if their borders are meeting the required standards.

Until then, the European Commission continues to work on the technical infrastructure – Schengen Information System that stores information about details and missing and wanted persons and stolen cars as well as arrest orders and visas throughout Europe.

Then, a visitor to Schengen countries will be able to visit these ten countries in addition of the current members using the same unique visa (if necessary).

In addition, Switzerland, which is surrounded by EU countries but isn't a member, will also join Schengen space next year.

This update is as of now. Granting of visas will be at the descretion of respective embassies.
