UDAAN UPDATE


For an Employment Work Visa Renewal ( X - Category )

 

It is informed to all our respected Travel Agents that UDAAN also provides the FRRO services even without the Personal Presence of the applicant.

 The Documents required for the FRRO are as follows:-

1.) Photocopy of the Passport
2.) Photocopy of the Visa Page
3.) Request for the Visa Extension on the Company's letter head stating that the company undertakes full responsibility of the passenger. The letter should be addressed to:-

 The Deputy Secretary
Foreigners Regional Registration Office
Jaisalmer House, Mansingh Road
New Delhi-110008

4.) Employment Contract
5.) Copy of the Residential permit
6.) Rental lease agreement
7.) Tax Paper/ IT returns and Form 16
