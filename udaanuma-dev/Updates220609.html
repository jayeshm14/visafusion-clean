MALAYSIA VISA - UPDATE

Dear Travel Partners;

Kindly note, the Validity of Malaysian Passport has been extended to 9 months for visa application. The six months passport validity is a requirement for entry and their minimum visa validity is three months. Thus making a total of 9 months required for Malaysian Visa Application.
