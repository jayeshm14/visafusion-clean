AUSTRALIA

As you are aware, we receive a set of documents for Australian visa from you. The same are submitted to the VFS - Australia. These are received by the Australian High Commission and they in their own wisdom may ask for any additional documents other than those submitted. It has been ruled by the VFS - Australia that they will charge an additional sum of Rs. 182/- for documents submitted to them for onward submission to the Australian High Commission. This amount will be bill to you on occurance.
