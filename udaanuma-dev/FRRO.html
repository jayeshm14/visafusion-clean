OCI / PIO / FRRO ASSISTANCE

Dear Travel Partners,
We would like to inform you that we also provide assistance for OCI/PIO/FRRO cases without the personal
presence of the applicant.

The details and documents are as follows:-

FRRO SERVICES- EXTENSION OF WORK VISA
1.) Photocopy of the Passport
2.) Photocopy of the Visa Page
3.) Request for the Visa Extension on the Company's letter head stating that the company undertakes full
responsibility of the passenger. The letter should be addressed to:-

The Deputy Secretary
Foreigners Regional Registration Office
Jaisalmer House, Mansingh Road
New Delhi-110008

4.) Employment Contract
5.) Copy of the Residential permit
6.) Rental lease agreement
7.) Tax Paper/ IT returns and Form 16
======================================================================

FRRO SERVICES-EXTENSION OF BUSINESS VISA
1.) Photocopy of the Passport
2.) Photocopy of the Visa Page
3.) Request for the Visa Extension on the Business letter head stating that the reason for the extension of Visa.
The letter should be addressed to:-
The Deputy Secretary
Foreigners Regional Registration Office
Jaisalmer House, Mansingh Road
New Delhi-110008

4.) Accomodation proof in India
5.) Undertraking from the Invitee Indian Company
======================================================================

OCI CARD ASSISTANCE-Documents Required
1.) OCI FORM-The applicant can be made online my visiting http://www.mha.nic.in/
2.) Original Passport with valid Indian Visa
3.) Cancelled Indian passport of the Applicant or of the Mother Or Father
4.) Birth Certificate of the Applicant or of the Mother Or Father
5.) DD of Rs 12650 in  Favor of P&AO(Main Secretariat) MEA incase the applicant holds a PIO Card then the DD will be of Rs 1250
======================================================================= PIO CARD ASSISTANCE-Documents Required

1.) PIO form
2.) Copy of Valid Passport and Visa
3.) Copy of Residential Permit & Registration Certificate 
4.) Cancelled Indian Passport OR the birth certificate OR Mother/Father Indian Passport copy
5.) DD of Rs 15000 in  Favor of FRRO,Delhi. Incase the applicant is minor then the Fee will be Rs 7500
5.) Husband OR Wifes Indian passport and Marriage Certificate issued by the Indian Authorities
