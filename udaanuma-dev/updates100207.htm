Swiss - Update


Since UDAAN INDIA PVT. LTD. has been on the VIP Agent List in the Swiss Embassy, following instructions have been announced by the the Embassy to make the Visa Process Smooth and Easy.

" Photograph of the applicant should be glued but not stapled/ pinned.

" The Visa Form should be strictly signed by the applicant.

" All the fields have to be filled with specifications given to Occupation for e.g "Business" is not sufficient Correct way: Self Employed , proprietor of an Export House

" Main purpose to Travel should be specific as well for e.g: Tourism ~ tour in Switzerland ( indicate itenary) Business ~ Training program with XYZ organization.

" Only the needed and the relevant documents have to be submitted, the ones required for the Visa Requirement list, for e.g, latest 3 months bank statements till date, current one I.T returns , registration of the company and partnership deed.

" First & last page copy of the passport is also compulsorily needed.

" Urgent or same day Visa requests would strictly not be entertained by the Embassy.

" If the passenger plans to visit any other European Country before or after visiting Switzerland, he has to obtain a visa for that particular country before applying for the Swiss Visa.

" All recent ( last 2 years) refusal stamps should be accompanied by a written explanation, if possible of the competent authority who put the tamp.

*** If any one does not follow these instructions the Visa Officer at the counter can refuse to take in the application form, since it would be considered incomplete.
