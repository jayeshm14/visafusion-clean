Updates

22 April 2008

  
 



ATTENTION TRAVELLERS TO SPAIN :-

This is in reference & a second reminder of our update that was sent on 26th February 2008.


The Spanish Embassy has directed that while submitting the documents the passport must be accompanied with the photocopy of all pages of the booklet. Incase the copies of the passport are not sent then an extra charge of Rs 100 will be levied per case/Passport booklet. Kindly do the needful to avoid any delay in submission of your case.


EMBASSY OF PORTUGAL:-

This is to make you aware that the Embassy of Portugal will be closed till 1st May 2008 as they are shifting their premises. Hence during this period there will be no submission or collection.

  
EMBASSY OF REPUBLIC OF HUNGARY:-

With immediate effect the Embassy of the Republic of Hungary has revised its Visa fee. Applicants will now have to pay a fee of Rs 3760.
